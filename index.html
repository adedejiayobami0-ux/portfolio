UX Redesign · AI

Claude Language & Usage Design

Rethinking how Claude communicates limits and language

5UX flows
40+Languages
+

I use Claude every day. And I kept noticing the same friction points: language switching required re-prompting every single session, and usage limits appeared as dead ends with no context or escape hatch.

So I did what PMs do — I redesigned it. This prototype proposes five distinct UX improvements: a persistent language pill next to the model picker, auto-detect banners that fire when you write in a different language, an always-visible usage meter with three states (healthy, warning, critical), and a hard-limit screen that gives exact resume times and a model-downgrade escape rather than a dead end.

The insight: users don't hate limits — they hate surprises. Transparency and control change everything.

05
Consumer · Fitness

Stride — Fitness Tracker for Runners

Dashboard, run tracking, workout logging & training plans

5Core screens
0Dependencies
+

Runners and gym-goers needed a single app that didn't force them to choose between tracking runs or logging workouts. Stride unifies both in one clean, fast experience — no install, no build step, just open and go.

The product covers the full training loop: a weekly stats dashboard with streak tracking, route visualisation with pace and split analysis, a live workout logger with set/rep checkoff, a training calendar showing planned vs actual volume, and a progress screen tracking PRs and 5K trends over time.

Built as a single HTML file with zero dependencies — a deliberate constraint that forced every design and engineering decision to be intentional. Roadmap includes GPS tracking, AI-powered plan generation, and Strava sync.
